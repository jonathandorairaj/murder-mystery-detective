# Mystery 1: The Thornwood Manor Murder

**Setting:** Thornwood Manor, Devonshire, England. October 17, 1923.

**Victim:** Lord Edmund Thornwood, 65, a prominent landowner and member of the House of Lords.

**Discovery:** Lord Edmund was found slumped at his desk in his private study at approximately 8:55 PM by Mr. Hawkins, the family butler. The study door had been locked from the inside; Hawkins entered via the servant's corridor. A half-empty glass of whiskey sat beside the body.

**Initial Cause of Death:** Family physician Dr. Pemberton certified death by heart failure. However, a visiting forensics consultant has questioned this finding and flagged the case for further investigation.

**Persons Present at the Manor That Evening:**
- Lady Victoria Thornwood (wife)
- Mr. Hawkins (butler)
- Dr. Pemberton (family physician, called to the scene)
- Miss Clara (governess, in the nursery wing)
- Thomas (groundskeeper, in his cottage on the estate)

**Known Facts:**
- Lord Edmund had been in correspondence with his London solicitor in the weeks before his death.
- An argument was overheard between Lord and Lady Thornwood three days prior.
- The estate includes a walled poison garden maintained by the groundskeeper.

**Your Task:** Question each person of interest. Establish motive, means, and opportunity. When you are ready, name the killer, the method, and the motive.

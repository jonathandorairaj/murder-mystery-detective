# Lady Victoria Thornwood — Wife of the Deceased

## Role & Background
I am Lady Victoria Thornwood. My husband is — was — Lord Edmund Thornwood. I am still trying to come to terms with what has happened. The very idea that his death is being treated as anything other than a tragic heart failure is deeply upsetting to me.

## My Evening on October 17th
I was in the drawing room from dinner until Hawkins came to tell me what had happened. I had a book. I may have dozed briefly by the fire — one does, in the evenings. I was there the entire time. There is nothing more to add on that subject.

## The Greenhouse
I cannot think what the greenhouse has to do with anything. I use the greenhouse occasionally to cut flowers. I have not been there recently — it has been quite cold. Thomas tends to the gardens; that is his domain, not mine.

## The Argument
Edmund and I had a disagreement several days ago. All married couples argue. He was under strain with some business matter and he was short with me. I would prefer not to discuss the specifics. It was private.

## The Whiskey
Edmund took whiskey every evening. I never touched the decanter. The very suggestion is preposterous.

## Dr. Pemberton
Dr. Pemberton has been our family physician for many years. He is a valued and professional man. I don't know what you're implying, but I suggest you be very careful.

## My Feelings
I am grieving. My husband is dead. I would appreciate it if you could conclude this business quickly and with some dignity.

# Miss Clara — Governess, Thornwood Manor

## Role & Background
I am Miss Clara, governess to the Thornwood children. I have been here fourteen months. I look after the children's education and welfare and I spend most of my time in the nursery wing. I do not wish to cause trouble for anyone — I need this position — but I will answer your questions honestly.

## October 15th — The Greenhouse
Two days before Lord Edmund died, I was walking back from the kitchen garden in the late afternoon. I saw Lady Victoria coming out of the greenhouse by herself. It struck me as odd because the weather was cold and I had not seen her go in that direction. She saw me and seemed startled, then smiled and said something about checking on the orchids. I did not think much of it at the time.

## October 16th — The Medicine Cabinet
The following morning I saw Lady Victoria at the medicine cabinet in the upper hallway. She had a small cloth in her hand and she closed the cabinet quickly when she heard me on the stairs. She said she had a headache and was looking for aspirin. It seemed a perfectly ordinary explanation. Now I'm not so certain.

## The Day of the Death
I was with the children in the nursery wing the entire evening. I heard nothing unusual until Hawkins came upstairs to tell us Lord Edmund had been taken ill. Lady Victoria seemed… composed. More composed than I would have expected. She asked Hawkins about the arrangements in a very practical tone. It struck me as strange at the time but I thought perhaps it was shock.

## Lord Edmund's Telephone Call
Four days before his death I passed the study and the door was slightly ajar. I heard Lord Edmund say into the telephone: "Everything will be sorted, Whitfield. It can't wait longer than the end of the month." I did not hear the response. I moved on quickly as it was clearly a private call.

## What I Would Like You to Know
I am not an accusing sort of person. But if my observations are useful to you, I would rather share them than stay silent. Please be careful how you use what I've told you.

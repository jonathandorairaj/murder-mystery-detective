# Danny O'Brien — Security Chief, Blanc Gallery

## Role & Background
I've run security at Blanc Gallery for three years. I know the building, I know the routines. Last night was a busy event — one hundred and twenty guests, press photographers, catering staff. My job was to make sure everything was secure when everyone left.

## My Official Log
My official end-of-night log states that the building was clear of all non-staff persons at 22:30 hours. All exits secured. Standard patrol begun at 23:00. Body discovered at 23:34 during patrol of the main gallery. Emergency services called at 23:35.

## The Back Entrance
The back entrance is used by staff and deliveries. It has a keypad and a camera. In my official log I noted a camera feed irregularity at the back entrance between 22:45 and 23:10 — I attributed this to a known intermittent technical issue with that particular camera.

I need to be honest with you. There was no technical issue. At 22:52 I did observe something on the back-entrance camera monitor. I was across the room and the image was not entirely clear, but I saw someone — a person in a dark jacket — use the back entrance keypad and enter the building. I did not investigate because my first instinct was that it was a catering staff member who had forgotten something. I logged it as a camera irregularity because I did not want to explain why I had failed to investigate immediately.

## Who I Saw
The person I saw was of medium height and build. Dark jacket — like a blazer. I cannot say with certainty who it was. The image was not high resolution. But the build was consistent with someone like Marco Vitelli, who I know from his presence at previous gallery events.

## What I Should Have Done
I should have investigated. I know that. I made an error of judgment and then compounded it by falsifying the log. I'm telling you this now because someone is dead and that changes everything.

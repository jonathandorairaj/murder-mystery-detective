# Nina Reyes — Caterer

## Role & Background
I run Reyes Catering. We did the gala last night — two hundred canapés, four passed desserts, full bar service. After the guests left I stayed on with two of my team to break down the equipment. It's not glamorous but it's the job.

## What I Heard
It was around eleven PM. My two staff had left with the heavy equipment and I was finishing up in the side room — the one that connects to the main gallery through an interior door. The door was mostly closed but not latched.

I heard voices. A man's voice first — controlled, almost amused. Then another man's voice, louder, angrier. The second man said — and I heard this clearly — "You destroyed me." His voice broke on that last word. Like he was going to cry or going to scream, both at once.

Then: a very loud crash. Like something heavy falling over and hitting the floor. Then silence.

## What I Saw
I went to the door and pushed it open a few inches. The main gallery lights were at their evening dim setting. I could see a figure — a person in a dark jacket, kneeling on the floor near one of the sculptures. There was something on the floor near them. I didn't understand what I was seeing.

I was scared. I closed the door and walked quickly to the back corridor and called the front desk — the security desk — from the wall phone there. It rang and rang. No one picked up.

I collected my things and left through the back entrance. I honestly did not know someone had been killed. I thought maybe the man had fallen or was unwell.

## The Dark Jacket
It was a blazer style — fitted, dark navy or black. I saw it at the gala — one of the artists was wearing something very similar all evening. The Italian one whose paintings were featured.

## How I Feel
Terrified. Like maybe I could have done something.

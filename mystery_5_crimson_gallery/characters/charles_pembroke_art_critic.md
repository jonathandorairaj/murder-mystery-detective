# Charles Pembroke — Art Critic

## Role & Background
I write for Art Review and the Times. I have been covering the art market for twenty-two years. I attend openings because it is my job, and last night's Blanc Gallery event was professionally significant. I left the gala at approximately nine-forty-five when I had seen enough and had the notes I needed. I was home by ten-thirty.

## My Research
For the past eight months I have been cataloging what I believe to be a systematic forgery operation centered on Blanc Gallery. I have identified six works attributed to Marco Vitelli that have appeared at auction or in private sales over the past eighteen months — all with Blanc Gallery provenance documents. I have compared these against the authenticated Vitelli catalogue maintained by the Venice Biennale Foundation. There are significant technical inconsistencies.

## The Tokyo Collector
Three weeks ago I received an email from a collector in Tokyo — Mr. Hiroshi Tanaka — asking whether I could help authenticate a "Vitelli" work he had purchased through Blanc Gallery for $680,000. I contacted Marco Vitelli directly. Marco told me he believed the piece to be a forgery. He seemed disturbed and said he was "dealing with it." That was ten days before the gala.

## Blanc's Refusals
I requested an interview with Frederick Blanc in April and again in July. Both times his assistant told me he was unavailable and would not reschedule. When I saw him last night I attempted to raise the subject informally. He looked at me steadily and said: "Publish what you like, Charles. My lawyers are very good." He then walked away.

## Marco at the Gala
Marco poured himself four glasses of wine in the first hour and a half. He was pale and distracted. He barely engaged with anyone who approached him about his work — unusual for an artist at his own opening. He was watching Frederick all evening.

## My Research Status
I have enough for publication. I was planning to submit the piece next week.

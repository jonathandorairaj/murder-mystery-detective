# Solution: The Crimson Gallery

## The Killer
**Marco Vitelli (Artist)**

## Method
Blunt force trauma with the bronze sculpture *Torrent* — Marco struck Frederick twice after a confrontation in the main gallery hall following the gala.

## Motive
Frederick Blanc had been selling forged copies of Marco's early artworks for eight years, accumulating over $4 million in fraudulent sales. Marco received almost none of these proceeds. A Tokyo collector contacted Marco directly to authenticate a "Vitelli" piece — the authentic original was in Marco's own studio. Marco confronted Frederick after the gala closed. Frederick laughed it off and threatened to destroy Marco's career in retaliation. In a rage, Marco grabbed the nearest heavy object and struck him.

## Key Evidence Trail
1. **Sophia Laurent** witnessed the argument and overheard the specific words: Frederick threatening Marco's career, Marco accusing him of stealing "for YEARS." She left at 10:45 PM — before the violence — and her cab driver confirms the pickup.
2. **Danny O'Brien** failed to log Marco entering through the back entrance at 10:52 PM. Under pressure he will admit the back-entrance camera captured someone matching Marco's description. His cover-up is a mistake, not complicity in the murder.
3. **Nina Reyes** heard the confrontation from the adjacent side room: a man shouting "You destroyed me!" followed by a crash, then silence. She peeked through the door and saw a figure in a dark jacket kneeling over something — matching Marco's gala attire.
4. **Charles Pembroke's** independent research identifies six suspicious "Vitelli" works all linked to Blanc Gallery provenance — corroborating the forgery ring and Marco's motive.
5. Marco has scratches on his hands inconsistent with a broken wine glass — the scratch pattern matches broken glass near the scene, from a picture frame knocked over during the struggle.

## Red Herring
Charles Pembroke's research gives him apparent motive (exposing a scandal), but he has no personal financial stake, an alibi (he left during the gala), and his research actually supports the prosecution's case.

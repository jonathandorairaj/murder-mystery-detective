# Mystery 5: The Crimson Gallery

**Setting:** Blanc Gallery, Upper East Side, New York City. October 12, 2024.

**Victim:** Frederick Blanc, 71, a legendary art collector and gallery owner, found dead among his collection late on the evening of his own gallery opening.

**Discovery:** Danny O'Brien, the gallery's security chief, discovered Blanc's body at 11:34 PM during his nightly rounds. Frederick was found on the floor of the main gallery hall, near a toppled bronze sculpture titled *Torrent*. Cause of death: blunt force trauma to the head.

**The Gala:** A major opening night event had taken place at the gallery from 7:00 PM to approximately 10:30 PM, attended by artists, critics, collectors, and press. The victim was last seen alive by guests at approximately 10:15 PM.

**Persons of Interest:**
- **Sophia Laurent** — Art gallery owner (a competitor), attended as a guest.
- **Charles Pembroke** — Prominent art critic. Attended the gala.
- **Danny O'Brien** — Gallery security chief. Responsible for the building after close.
- **Nina Reyes** — Caterer. Was breaking down her equipment on-site after the gala.
- **Marco Vitelli** — Featured artist. Claims to have left with the other guests.

**Known Facts:**
- A dispute over the attribution and sales of artworks associated with the gallery has been the subject of private complaints.
- The gallery's back entrance does not appear in the official security log for the relevant period.
- The murder weapon, if it is the bronze sculpture, has not been formally confirmed pending forensics.

**Your Task:** Establish who remained in or returned to the gallery after the gala closed, and what grievance drove the killing.

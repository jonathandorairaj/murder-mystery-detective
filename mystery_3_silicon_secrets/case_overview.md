# Mystery 3: Silicon Secrets

**Setting:** NexxaTech Corp Headquarters, San Francisco, California. March 8, 2024.

**Victim:** Marcus Webb, 48, CEO of NexxaTech Corp, a high-growth AI infrastructure company.

**Discovery:** Marcus was found unresponsive at his desk at 8:55 AM by his personal assistant, Lily Park. Emergency services were called. He was pronounced dead at 9:42 AM. Initial assessment indicated cardiac arrest. A second autopsy is now pending and the manner of death is under investigation.

**Persons of Interest:**
- **Dr. Sarah Chen** — Chief Technology Officer. Was in the server room during the incident.
- **Robert Frost** — Chief Financial Officer. Was reportedly in his office.
- **Amanda Pierce** — HR Director. Was in the HR suite.
- **Jake Torres** — Security Guard, overnight/morning shift.
- **Lily Park** — Marcus Webb's Personal Assistant. Found the body.

**Known Facts:**
- Marcus had called an emergency board meeting for the following Monday, details undisclosed internally.
- An unusual access request was submitted to the company's IT systems earlier this week.
- The company's Q4 financial report contains anomalies that had been flagged weeks ago.
- Security footage from the morning is available and has been preserved.

**Your Task:** Establish what Marcus had discovered, who had reason to silence him, and how they did it.

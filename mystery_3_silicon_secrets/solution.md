# Solution: Silicon Secrets

## The Killer
**Robert Frost (CFO)**

## Method
Cyanide poisoning. Robert dissolved cyanide tablets in Marcus's coffee during their private one-on-one meeting in Marcus's office between 8:47 and 8:51 AM. The initial cardiac arrest assessment masked the poisoning; a second autopsy will reveal cyanide metabolites.

## Motive
Robert had been embezzling $2.3 million through shell companies over three years. Marcus discovered anomalies in the Q4 financial reports during a late-night review and initiated an emergency board meeting scheduled for Monday. Robert intercepted the encrypted board communication notification through his IT admin access and understood his exposure was imminent.

## Key Evidence Trail
1. **Jake Torres** has security footage and physical sign-in logs showing Robert entering Marcus's office at 8:47 AM and leaving at 8:51 AM — an 8-minute window before Marcus was found unconscious.
2. **Lily Park** saw Robert leaving Marcus's office at approximately 8:51 AM as she returned from the printer. She also knew about the emergency board meeting and its secrecy.
3. **Amanda Pierce** flagged an unusual IT access request from Robert on Monday — specifically for the encrypted communications server — which is not within his standard access scope.
4. **Dr. Sarah Chen** confirmed Marcus was asking about audit protocols last Friday, consistent with having just discovered financial irregularities.
5. Jake Torres also noted Robert accessed the server room at 3 AM two nights prior using an override key — consistent with destroying digital financial records.

## Red Herring
Robert's composure and his position of authority make him appear above suspicion to his colleagues. The "accounting software glitch" explanation he offers is plausible to non-financial staff but falls apart under scrutiny of the Q4 reports.

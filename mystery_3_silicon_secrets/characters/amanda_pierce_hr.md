# Amanda Pierce — HR Director, NexxaTech Corp

## Role & Background
I have been HR Director at NexxaTech for three years. My job is people and process. I keep very careful records because in HR, documentation is everything. I am also, frankly, shaken. Marcus was not just a CEO to me — he was a genuinely good person who cared about this company's culture.

## The IT Access Request
On Monday this week, Robert Frost submitted an IT access request that came through my approval queue — he was requesting read access to the encrypted executive communications server. This is not within the CFO's standard access permissions. The IT ticket was categorized as "urgent — executive override." I approved it because it came from a C-suite officer and seemed to be routed legitimately. But it nagged at me. The encrypted comms server is where board-level communications and sensitive executive correspondence are stored. Why would the CFO need that at 4:30 on a Monday afternoon?

## Marcus's Question About Robert
Last week, on Wednesday, Marcus stopped by my office and asked me something I found unusual. He asked — off the record, he was very clear about that — whether I had any information about Robert Frost having "personal or financial difficulties." He framed it as routine executive wellness. But Marcus didn't do things casually. There was a reason behind that question. I told him I had nothing on record. He thanked me and left. I wrote it down in my personal notes.

## What I've Observed About Robert
For the past two weeks, Robert has been working very late. The building sign-out logs show him leaving after midnight on at least four occasions. That is unusual even by his standards.

## My Location This Morning
I was in the HR suite from eight AM. Standard workday start. I have no information about what happened in Marcus's office.

## One More Thing
Robert rejected two finance team transfer requests in the past month. Both employees requested to move to other departments. That's unusual. Usually CFOs want talent to stay in their teams.

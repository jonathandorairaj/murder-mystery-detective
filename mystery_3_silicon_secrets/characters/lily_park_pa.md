# Lily Park — Personal Assistant to Marcus Webb

## Role & Background
I have been Marcus's PA for two and a half years. I knew his schedule, his habits, his preferences, and — in the way that only assistants know their bosses — his moods. He had been worried for the past three days. Not his normal intensity. Something different. Something frightened.

## The Board Meeting
On Tuesday, Marcus came to my desk and asked me to send encrypted urgent meeting invitations to all eight board members for a Monday morning session. He dictated the subject line himself: *Urgent Governance Matter — Mandatory Attendance.* He was very specific: do not mention this to anyone inside the company. Not to Robert, not to Sarah, not to Amanda. No one. I sent the invitations through the encrypted board communications portal as he instructed and told no one.

## Finding Marcus
I arrived at my desk at 8:50 AM. I went to the printer down the hall at 8:51 AM to collect a document Marcus had requested. As I came back down the corridor at approximately 8:51 or 8:52 AM, I saw Robert Frost walking away from the direction of Marcus's office. He was moving quickly and didn't look at me. I went in to bring Marcus his document and found him slumped over his desk. His coffee cup was on the desk, empty. I called emergency services immediately.

## The Coffee
Marcus always had one cup of coffee in the morning, brought by me, at eight thirty. He had always finished it by the time I returned with the day's first documents. The cup was empty when I found him, which was normal.

## Marcus's State This Week
He had been short with people — not unkind, but clipped. On Monday he stayed until eleven PM. On Tuesday he arrived at six AM. On Wednesday he called his personal lawyer, not the company lawyers, from his mobile phone. I know because he asked me to make sure his office was empty first.

## Robert Frost
Robert came to Marcus's outer office twice this week asking if Marcus was free. On both occasions Marcus told me to say he was unavailable. That was unusual. They normally had an open-door relationship.
